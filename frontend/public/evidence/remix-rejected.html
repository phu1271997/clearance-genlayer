Track description

A 58-second edit produced as the soundtrack for the Vodka Nord national advertising campaign. The hook is the sampled vocal line "hold the line, hold the line" taken directly from "Neon Rain" by Mira Solvang, pitched up and looped across the whole spot. Approximately 22 seconds of the original recording are used in total.
