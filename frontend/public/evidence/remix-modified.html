Long Exposure (Neon Rain rework)

by KVSTLE · released 2026 · downtempo · 5:02

Track description

Long Exposure is built around a 12-second instrumental loop lifted from "Neon Rain" by Mira Solvang, sampled continuously from 0:48 to 1:00. The loop carries the entire rhythm section of this rework and repeats throughout the track. The sample is instrumental only — the vocal hook from the original is not used.
