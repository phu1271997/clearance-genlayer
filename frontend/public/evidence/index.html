Demo evidence pages

Clearance adjudicates on-chain: GenLayer validators call gl.nondet.web.render on the URLs stored in a work and a claim, then weigh that page text against the artist's natural-language licence. These four pages are stable, readable public URLs published by the dApp so anyone can reproduce each verdict path end to end. Any real public URL works exactly the same way.

  • Neon Rain — Mira Solvang original Use as source_url when registering the work. States the sampling policy the licence text mirrors.

    /evidence/original-neon-rain.html

  • Halogen (Neon Rain edit) expects APPROVED 3-second instrumental loop, credited, no advertising. Under the 4-second free threshold, so a 0% proposed split satisfies the licence.

    /evidence/remix-approved.html

  • Long Exposure (Neon Rain rework) expects MODIFIED 12-second instrumental loop, credited. The licence requires 25% for samples of 4–15 seconds, so an under-proposed split gets corrected upward.

    /evidence/remix-modified.html

  • Hold The Line (Vodka Nord cut) expects REJECTED Vocal hook sampled and used in an alcohol advertisement — two independent licence violations. Deposit is forfeited.

    /evidence/remix-rejected.html

Mira Solvang, KVSTLE and Vodka Nord are fictional.
